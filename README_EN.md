## 🚀 Personal Projects

Projects I led, ordered by repository creation date:

### [**Ancient_Books**](https://github.com/Fyuan0206/Ancient_Books) · 2024-08

**Background**: An entry in the Chinese Collegiate Computing Competition · **National Second Prize**

**Problem addressed**: Classical Chinese poetry, prose, idioms, and allusions can be difficult to understand and often require consulting multiple sources. Built for researchers, students, and enthusiasts of traditional Chinese culture, this project provides an all-in-one toolkit for poetry appreciation, classical Chinese translation, idiom explanations, and annotations of *The Analects* and *Hundred Family Surnames*.

**Tech stack**: Domain fine-tuning of InternLM2-7B · RAG over classical Chinese corpora · Task-specific prompt templates for poetry appreciation, classical Chinese translation, idioms, *The Analects*, and *Hundred Family Surnames*

---

### [**Xinjing-LM**](https://github.com/Kedreamix/Xinjing-LM) · 2025-02

**Background**: An entry in the GDC 2025 DeepSeek-Qwen Model Distillation Challenge · **Third Place**

**Problem addressed**: Demand for mental health support is growing while professional resources remain limited, and general-purpose LLMs often lack domain knowledge and safety-aware conversational capabilities. This project provides multi-turn empathetic dialogue and informed guidance for users seeking emotional support and psychological advice.

**Tech stack**: Psychology-domain fine-tuning of Qwen · Multi-source data fusion and DeepSeek R1 data distillation · Multi-turn dialogue construction across emotion types and life scenarios · Hybrid LoRA and full-parameter fine-tuning

---

### [**SelfAgent**](https://github.com/Fyuan0206/SelfAgent) · 2026-01

**Background**: An AI healthcare project

**Problem addressed**: Professional psychological counseling can be scarce, expensive, and difficult to access when support is needed. This project provides around-the-clock AI emotional companionship, combining text, voice, and image inputs to recognize 12 core DBT emotions. It routes users through L1 casual conversation, L2 DBT intervention, or L3 crisis response, and recommends evidence-based skills such as TIPP and STOP alongside 24-hour support resources.

**Tech stack**: A CAMEL-AI primary agent equipped with emotion detection, DBT skills, crisis protocol, and user profile tools · Multimodal input fusion → risk classification → L1 conversation / L2 skill recommendation / L3 crisis intervention

---

### [**Myietls – KanYa**](https://github.com/Fyuan0206/myietls) · 2026-03

**Background**: An entry in the ModelScope AI Hackathon Tour

**Problem addressed**: IELTS candidates often struggle to access affordable speaking practice and mock-test feedback. The platform covers Part 1 question-bank practice, AI-powered speaking mock tests, instant scoring, and mistake tracking, supporting a complete learning workflow for students, teachers, and administrators.

**Tech stack**: CAMEL IELTS Agent for multi-turn mock-test dialogue and four-dimensional speaking assessment · Agentic RAG retrieving Part 1 questions by band score · Voice capture → STT transcription → agent evaluation and feedback → TTS playback

---

### [**GoGoGo, Let's Go!**](https://github.com/Fyuan0206/GoGoGo) · 2026-05

**Background**: An entry in the Meituan Campus AI Hackathon

**Problem addressed**: Planning an in-depth trip requires combining fragmented information from travel guides, reviews, weather, transportation, and other sources. From a single natural-language request, the project generates three executable itineraries for an immersive journey along Gansu's Silk Road: landscape, photography, and classic loop routes.

**Tech stack**: An A0 orchestrator coordinating 12 specialist agents for intent, profiling, guides, POIs, reviews, dining, accommodation, transportation, weather, routing, and quality assurance · SSE streaming of agent progress · POI scoring, TrustScore, and route optimization producing three executable plans

---

### [**LearnFlow**](https://github.com/Fyuan0206/LearnFlow) · 2026-06

**Background**: An entry in the Agent Builder Hackathon Shenzhen · **Second Prize**

**Problem addressed**: Technical learners face an overwhelming amount of material and often struggle to filter, structure, and turn it into action. Given a learning topic, the project automatically gathers resources, generates knowledge cards and graphs, plans a staged learning path, and breaks key points into actionable tasks.

**Tech stack**: LLM-orchestrated service pipeline · Topic input → resource discovery and AI summaries → knowledge cards / graph construction → staged learning path planning → actionable task breakdown

---

### [**ArguMesh**](https://github.com/Fyuan0206/ArguMesh) · 2026-08

**Background**: A personal open-source AI research workbench

- [Website](https://argumesh.nekocfy.com/)
- [WeChat community](https://github.com/Fyuan0206/ArguMesh/issues/4)

**Problem addressed**: Close reading, evidence checking, research questions, and paper writing are often scattered across PDF readers, spreadsheets, notes, and chat-style AI, making sources easy to lose and hard to compare across papers. Built for researchers, graduate students, and paper authors, ArguMesh connects literature reading → evidence matrices → research threads → experiment planning → LaTeX writing into a continuous, local-first, evidence-traceable workflow.

**Tech stack**: React 19 + TypeScript + Vite 6 · Hono local API · SQLite / Drizzle · Tauri desktop shell · pdf.js + OCR · Optional Research Agent (OpenAI / Anthropic-compatible APIs)
